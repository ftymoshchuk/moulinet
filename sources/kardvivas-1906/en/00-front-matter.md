> **Translator's note.** Hungarian puts the family name first, so the author appears on the book as "Leszák Károly" (in Western order, Károly Leszák). *Honvéd* is the Royal Hungarian Landwehr, the Hungarian home-defence force of Austria-Hungary; the Ludovika Academy was its officer-training academy in Budapest.

> **Translator's notes on the contents.**
> - The Part One heading reads "Individual training in a group" here (*Egyes kiképzés csoportban*) but just "Individual training" on the part's own title page (book p. 3). Both are as printed.
> - Chapter VI has no numbered sections, so the contents give it no page; it falls between §35 (p. 45) and §36 (p. 51). Chapters XII and XIII carry no title in the contents either.
> - ‡ Several terms are uncertain until the sections are read. "Naturalist" (§65) probably means a fencer who fights by instinct rather than by the school's rules, but that is a guess. *Eltolás* (§29) is literally "pushing/shifting aside". *Elővágás* (§51 B) is literally "fore-cut" and may mean a cut that anticipates the attack. Italian and German terms in brackets (*cavazione*, *filo*, *deviamento*, *battuta*, *sforzo*, *appuntata*, *contratempó*, *Rimessen*, *riposten*, *assaut*, *ambó*) are kept as printed.
