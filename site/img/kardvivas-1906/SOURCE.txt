Figures from Leszák Károly, Kardvívás ("Sabre Fencing"), 2nd edition, Budapest, 1906.

These are copies of the files of the same names in sources/kardvivas-1906/figures/,
which were cropped from a scan of the book: 146 pages, with the stamps of the Országos
Széchényi Könyvtár (the Hungarian National Széchényi Library). The tests hold each
copy to be the same file as the original, and to say what that folder's manifest.json
says of it. The page shows them beside the postures they are photographs of.

The reuse terms of the book, of the scan and of the figures are not yet checked: see
the "Rights" section of sources/kardvivas-1906/README.md, and issue #33 of the
repository.
